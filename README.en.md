# World Myth Canon (Chinese translations)

This repository is a collection of Chinese EPUB editions of world myth and religious source texts. The base texts are public-domain works, or texts that communities have already made public. The Chinese was prepared with [Cursor](https://cursor.com/) from those bases, then laid out as books. These files are not official scripture of any religion. They are not a stand-in for the Chinese Union Version or for any other copyrighted Chinese translation.

中文：[README.md](README.md)

Reading order, reserved numbers, and unfinished titles: [阅读说明.md](阅读说明.md) (Chinese). Old-to-new number table: [NUMBERING.md](NUMBERING.md). Legal limits: [DISCLAIMER.md](DISCLAIMER.md).

## What is in the repo

There are **195** finished EPUBs with covers. Each filename is a three-digit number plus the title, for example `001 埃利都创世记.epub`. Numbers follow the original catalogue. Unfinished numbers stay empty. Later finished books were not shifted forward to fill those slots.

`附录/000 FGO神话人物总表.epub` is a Fate/Grand Order name index for these books. It is an appendix, not part of the 001 sequence.

The `_work/` drafts folder is not in this repository. Unsealed or incomplete EPUBs are not here either.

## What is not here

- **071 Mahabharata** keeps number 071. Translation stopped around Vana Parva section 155. The eighteen parvans are not complete. It is not in this repo. **072 Bhagavad Gita** remains 072.
- These titles paused mid-work and keep their original numbers, with no file in the repo: **166 Parzival**, **168 Le Morte d'Arthur**, **186 Chilam Balam**, **188 Florentine Codex**, **190 Huarochirí Manuscript**, **191 Diné Bahaneʼ**, **196 the Hiʻiaka and Pele epic**, **199 Lore of the Whare-wānanga**, **218 Pistis Sophia**. **196** is not the same book as the published **197 Pele and Hiʻiaka**.
- **180 Mvet** was cancelled. No qualifying public-domain field text was found. It does not take a number. 181 remains 181.
- Apart from **099 Shan Hai Jing**, the planned Chinese volumes (old 100-121 and 227-228) were not made and do not take numbers. After 099 the main sequence continues at **122 Kojiki**.

## How to read the numbers

Sort by filename to follow the original catalogue order. Empty numbers are either unfinished placeholders or planned skips. The full table is in [NUMBERING.md](NUMBERING.md). Details in Chinese are in [阅读说明.md](阅读说明.md).

## What these translations are

Reading a file is not initiation, empowerment, or any religious authorization. Tantras, the Picatrix, and similar works are here as narrative and history of religion. Reading is not practice. Australian First Nations pieces use only texts the communities have already made public.

Fate/Grand Order footnotes only mark names that appear in the game. They are a reading index, not scriptural commentary.
